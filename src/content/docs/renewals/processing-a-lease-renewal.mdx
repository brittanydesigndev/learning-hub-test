## Purpose

Use this when a resident has accepted a renewal offer and the new lease needs to be generated, signed and recorded.

## Before you start

- The resident’s acceptance is recorded in [Property management system]
- You have the approved term and rent from the offer
- All occupants on the current lease are confirmed

## Steps

<Walkthrough simulation="#try-it" doneTitle="Renewal complete" doneMessage="You’ve processed the renewal. The written steps stay on this page whenever you need a refresher.">

1. **Open the resident’s renewal record.** In [Property management system], go to [Menu path] and search by unit or resident name.

2. **Confirm the term and rent.** Match both to the accepted offer before you generate anything. If they differ, stop and follow [When the offer changes](#when-the-offer-changes).

3. **Generate the renewal lease.** Select [Lease template] and check that every occupant appears on the lease.

4. **Send for signature.** Send to all leaseholders and note the expected signing date on the record.

5. **Countersign and complete.** Once all signatures are in, countersign and mark the renewal complete.

</Walkthrough>

## When the offer changes

<Aside type="caution" title="Don’t edit the lease">
If the resident asks for a different term after accepting, send a revised offer first. See [Sending a renewal offer](../sending-a-renewal-offer/).
</Aside>
