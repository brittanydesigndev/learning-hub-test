## Purpose

Use this to send a renewal offer to a resident whose lease is approaching expiration.

## Steps

<Walkthrough doneTitle="Offer sent">

1. **Check the renewal window.** Confirm the lease is within [number] days of expiring.

2. **Pull the approved pricing.** Get the renewal rent and term options from [Pricing tool].

3. **Send the offer.** Use the [Renewal offer template] and send it to every leaseholder.

4. **Set a follow-up.** Add a reminder for [number] days out if there’s no response.

</Walkthrough>
