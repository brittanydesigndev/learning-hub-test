## Purpose

A day-of checklist so nothing gets missed when a resident moves in.

## Steps

<Walkthrough doneTitle="Move-in complete">

1. **Confirm the lease is fully signed.** Check [Property management system] for all signatures.

2. **Verify move-in funds.** Confirm the move-in balance is paid.

3. **Complete the unit inspection.** Walk the unit and record its condition in [Inspection tool].

4. **Hand over keys and access.** Issue keys, fobs and any amenity access.

</Walkthrough>
